## 4. Operating Schedule & Ordering Windows

- [ ] **Opening Time:** Standard: `10:00 AM` (Monday – Saturday)
- [ ] **Closing Time:** Standard: `10:00 PM`
- [ ] **Sunday Hours:** `12:00 PM – 9:00 PM` (or closed)
- [ ] **Last Order Cutoff:** 30 minutes before closing (`9:30 PM`)
- [ ] **Surge / Peak Demand Windows:** 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM (Lunch) & 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Dinner)

---

## 5. Kitchen & Counter Physical Setup

- [ ] **Kitchen Tablet:** At least one 10-inch Android or iPad tablet positioned at the prep counter running the KDS screen (`/kds`).
- [ ] **Counter Tablet / Phone:** Device at the pickup station for marking orders `DELIVERED` when customer collects.
- [ ] **Sound Alerts:** Audio notifications enabled on KDS tablet so kitchen staff hear when a new confirmed order arrives.
- [ ] **Packaging Supplies:** AY Pizza branded pizza boxes, foil wrap, poly bags, and receipts stickers.
- [ ] **Dispatch Rider Coordination:** Designated campus courier riders briefed on landmark pickup and room delivery protocols.

---

## 6. Cancellation, Refund & Customer Support Policy

- [ ] **Customer Support Contact:** Phone: `+234 80X XXX XXXX` | Email: `orders@aypizza.ng`
- [ ] **Cancellation Window:** Customers may only cancel before the kitchen marks the order `PREPARING`.
- [ ] **Kitchen Out-of-Stock Rejection:** If an ingredient runs out, staff cancels order in KDS providing a clear reason (`REJECTION_REASON_REQUIRED`), which updates `refund_status` to `REFUND_REQUIRED`.
- [ ] **Refund SLA:** Customer refunds processed via Paystack within 24–48 hours for cancelled paid orders.
